# Sole Story

Bespoke, hand-painted 1-of-1 Nike Air Force 1s made from your life story.

Answer a few questions — your team, your game, your pet, the things that make you you — and get 7 custom designs within 8 hours. Pick your top 3, spin them in 360°, choose your one, and a verified artist hand-paints it and ships it straight to your door.

**Live site:** https://vivekn4.github.io/sole-story/

**How it works**

1. Tell your story (2 mins)
2. 7 custom designs in 8 hours — 7 more in 2–5 hrs if you want changes
3. Pick your top 3 & spin in 360°
4. Hand-painted & shipped, tracked to your door

No two pairs alike. No mass production. Just your story, worn.
